FAQ

Got Questions?
We Got Answers.

Everything you need to know before you start cooking.

How does SNAP/EBT free access actually work? ⌄
We partner with a secure benefits verification service to confirm your SNAP enrollment — we never ask for your EBT card number. Once verified, your account is upgraded to Flame Rank at no cost for as long as you're enrolled. Verification renews annually. If you don't have SNAP benefits but are in financial hardship, you can also self-attest using our free hardship waiver form.
Is my camera or pantry data stored on your servers? ⌄
No. Camera images are processed in memory during the scan and immediately discarded — they are never uploaded or stored. Location data used for the SNAP store locator is also processed in real-time and never retained. We collect only what's necessary to provide the service. You can review our full data practices in the Privacy Policy.
Can I use FrameFeast on the web without downloading an app? ⌄
Yes — that's exactly where we're starting. The FrameFeast web app is launching first and supports all core features including the pantry scanner (via your browser's camera), the SNAP store locator, the full recipe library, and your XP progress. The iOS and Android apps are in development and will arrive later this year.
How does the XP and ranking system work? ⌄
Every recipe you complete awards XP based on difficulty, cook time, and bonus objectives. Accumulating XP increases your level and unlocks new characters, badges, and recipe packs. Daily challenges offer bonus XP and streak rewards. Badges range from Common to Legendary rarity. Your rank is displayed on your profile and the global leaderboard — anonymous by default, opt-in for public visibility.
What makes FrameFeast different from other recipe apps? ⌄
Most recipe apps are built for people who already have everything. FrameFeast is built around the reality of limited budgets, pantry constraints, and SNAP/EBT access — with the soul of an anime game layered on top. The combination of AI pantry scanning, real USDA SNAP store data, gamification, and original character-led storytelling doesn't exist anywhere else. This is the only app where cooking feels like leveling up.
